Learn to spot danger before it spots you. Free video training from Ian — narrated video lessons for everyday life in Trinidad & Tobago. Press play and learn.

Every night on Crime Watch, Ian sees the same thing: most trouble gives warning signs first. A car circling the block. A stranger watching the ATM a little too long. A gate left open “just for a minute.” These video lessons teach you to notice those signs early — and know what to do next.

It is completely free, for everybody. No payment, no account, no password. Watch it, share it with your family, sit down and watch it with your children. This is Ian’s gift to the public — because a safer street starts with people who are paying attention.
